# Research Stage

Date: 2026-05-28 KST

## Summary

The project is feasible as a constrained prototype, but not yet safe to promise as a finished production service. Meta DAT and Android XR provide credible glasses development paths. The unresolved core is reliable directional voice detection and glasses-side haptics.
