# Product Stage

Date: 2026-05-28 KST

## MVP

Voice Direction Glass detects a trusted enrolled speaker calling the user and emits a directional cue. The first implementation validates workflow and output, then proves hardware direction feasibility.

## Core Screens

- Session
- Speaker Enrollment
- Trigger Phrase Settings
- Alert Detail
- Device Lab
- Privacy Settings

## Guardrails

- Local-first voice profile.
- No raw audio by default.
- No exact direction claim under low confidence.
- No public release until preview SDK limits and privacy text are resolved.
